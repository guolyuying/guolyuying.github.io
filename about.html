About

Lyuying Guo

Hi! This is Lyuying, a 3D Character Artist with a background in illustration. I specialize in stylized modeling, texturing, and shader development. I love building 3D characters and worlds that look like my 2D drawing style.

Born and raised in mainland China, I moved to Los Angeles after high school to pursue my dream in animation at the University of Southern California (USC) where I unexpectedly discovered the world of game dev. I am currently pursuing an MFA in Animation at the Savannah College of Art and Design (SCAD) focusing on 3D stylzied look development and making my personal short film "Yours, Mr. Owl" (2027).
